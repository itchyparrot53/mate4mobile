# Mate4Mobile brand kit

**Every mile, in view.**

Mate4Mobile is a private daily companion for TeslaMate. The identity should feel composed, legible and precise. Inspiration comes from automotive interfaces; the brand itself is independent. Do not use Tesla’s T, Tesla’s wordmark or official product artwork as this project’s identity.

## Identity

- **Name:** Mate4Mobile. Keep the capitals and numeral in running text.
- **Compact name:** M4M. Use in small secondary contexts, never as the only introduction to the project.
- **Descriptor:** Your private TeslaMate companion.
- **Primary line:** Every mile, in view.
- **Supporting line:** Built for the drive. Designed for the glance.
- **Repository:** `itchyparrot53/mate4mobile`.

The symbol is a continuous road folded into an M, with a mint terminal. It reads as a route and a monogram at small sizes. The endpoint is part of the mark, not a live-status light.

Regenerate exports with `npm ci && npm run brand`. Wordmark and social typography are outlined from the bundled Inter font so the masters do not depend on installed fonts. SVG is the source; Sharp is used for deterministic format export, not to invent or modify photographs. Vehicle renders are third-party assets and are not regenerated by the brand build.

Mint is an accent, not a full-screen fill. For light backgrounds, use the supplied dark mark with `#367A60` accent. Warning/error colours remain semantic and distinct from the brand accent. Do not place white text on mint; use ink.

## Typography and layout

Inter is bundled under the SIL Open Font License. Use 400 for body, 500 for display headings and 550–600 for section headings. Body text is 16px or larger; ordinary labels are 14px or larger. Small timestamps, provenance and decorative eyebrow labels may be smaller. Large numbers should use tabular figures where values update.

Use generous space and a clear order: current reading → meaning → supporting detail. Prefer rows and dividers to a grid of competing cards. Desktop layouts can put map and chart side by side; phones use a natural vertical sequence and large touch targets.

## Logo rules

Maintain at least one stroke width of clear space. Symbol minimum: 16px; preferred interface size: 24px or larger. Wordmark minimum: 150px wide. Keep proportions and the supplied colours. Do not stretch, add shadows, redraw the numeral as a lightning bolt, or incorporate another company’s mark. Use a monochrome master when a background cannot support the two-colour mark.

The square icon artwork is intentionally unrounded; operating systems apply their own masks. Do not pre-cut the maskable asset into a circle or rounded square.

## Motion and voice

140ms for feedback, 240ms for transitions, 420ms for entrances. Use `cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1)` to settle smoothly. Honour `prefers-reduced-motion`; avoid decorative looping animations in core reading flows.

Write plainly: “Last recorded 12 minutes ago”, “Costs recorded for 8 of 9 sessions”, “Building your baseline”. Prefer explicit uncertainty to false precision. “Recorded” means stored by TeslaMate; “estimated” is not a diagnostic. Never label missing data as zero or imply that sleeping-car data is live.
