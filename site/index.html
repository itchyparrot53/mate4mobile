A few useful answers.

Do I need TeslaMate already?

Yes. Mate4Mobile is a separate viewer for an existing TeslaMate PostgreSQL database. It does not collect telemetry or replace the logger, Grafana or the official Tesla app.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

It is a responsive progressive web app. Open your private HTTPS address in Safari or Chrome and add it to your Home Screen. It also adapts to a desktop browser.

Can I control or wake the car?

No. The app reads recorded history. Vehicle controls stay in the official Tesla app; data freshness depends on what TeslaMate has recorded.

What does GitHub Pages host?

This showcase and an entirely synthetic demo. Your private dashboard runs on your own server. GitHub Pages never connects to your TeslaMate database.

Is this an official Tesla product?

No. Mate4Mobile is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Tesla or TeslaMate. Vehicle imagery belongs to Tesla, Inc. and/or its respective owners and is used for identification and illustration. See the rights notice below.
